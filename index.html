維修基本流程與作業內容 (Repair Flow)

嚴格控管收貨、拆解、檢測、組裝測試至品管包裝之標準作業流程
A controlled standard workflow covering receipt, disassembly, inspection, assembly and testing, quality control, and packaging.

STEP 1
收貨與拆解清潔 / Receiving, Disassembly & Cleaning

作業內容 / Work: 收貨記錄、不良檢測、客訴處理、拆解、矽輪除污、拋光研磨及表面噴砂。
Receiving records, defect inspection, customer complaint handling, disassembly, silicon rotor decontamination, polishing, and surface sandblasting.

環境要求 / Work Areas: 碼頭區、拆解室、清潔室、委外加工 / Loading area, disassembly room, cleaning room, and subcontracted processing.

STEP 2
檢測與組裝測試 / Inspection, Assembly & Testing

作業內容 / Work: 電路檢測與維修、馬達維修與功能測試、感測器檢修、零組件更換、組裝校正、運轉測試及耐久與耐溫測試。
Electrical inspection and repair, motor servicing and functional testing, sensor inspection, component replacement, assembly and calibration, operational testing, and endurance and temperature testing.

環境要求 / Work Areas: 加工區、無塵室 / Processing area and cleanroom.

STEP 3
品管、包裝與送貨 / Quality Control, Packaging & Delivery

作業內容 / Work: 真空氦氣測漏、記錄與標示、潔淨包裝、送貨記錄及客戶服務。
Helium leak testing, documentation and labeling, clean packaging, delivery records, and customer service.

環境要求 / Work Areas: 無塵室、碼頭區及客戶交貨地點 / Cleanroom, loading area, and customer delivery locations.

欣科國際有限公司簡介 / About Prima International

欣科國際有限公司創立於 2002 年，主要提供半導體與光電設備零件供應及真空閥門維修服務，並持續開發科技產品、提升服務品質，以滿足客戶全方位需求。
Founded in 2002, Prima International supplies parts for semiconductor and optoelectronics equipment and provides vacuum valve repair services. We continue to develop technology products and improve our service to meet our customers' needs.

經營方針 / Business Principles: 品質、服務、成本、效率 / Quality, Service, Cost, and Efficiency

公司發展歷史沿革 Timeline

1996：公司成立於新竹／竹北，並設立銷售服務部門。
Founded in Hsinchu and Zhubei and established the sales and service department.
1997：成立半導體真空閥件維修服務部門。
Established the semiconductor vacuum valve repair service department.
1999：開始銷售半導體零件與材料。
Began supplying semiconductor parts and materials.
2001：開始提供自動控制設計與服務。
Started providing automation control design and services.
2003：成立光電事業部門。
Established the optoelectronics division.
2004：成立中國蘇州維修中心。
Opened the Suzhou service center in China.
2006：遷至新竹市香山區新址。
Relocated to a new facility in Xiangshan District, Hsinchu City.
2007–2010：閥件維修拓展至國際市場，並全面導入電腦化管理。
Expanded valve repair services internationally and implemented computerized operations.
